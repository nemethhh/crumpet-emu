### Finding

**Important (P1)**

1. **`mtee_verify` ledger classification contradicts its own taxonomy, and the premise that bounds it is not test-pinned.** `crumpet_emu/preloader/seams.py:14-16` defines `oracle-conservative` as modeling outcomes "in the direction that can only make the emu MORE pessimistic than silicon … (fail/halt/verify-fail)". The mtee hook (`emu.py:546-550`) returns **always-OK**, which is fail-open/optimistic — the opposite direction. The citation (`seams.py:71-73`, `docs/guarantee.md:33`) bounds the exposure with two arguments: the crafted tee1 "aborts its read before any verify", and genuine donor tee2 would pass on silicon. Neither is asserted anywhere (grep for `mtee` in tests matches only the ledger test) — if the ordering premise were wrong, the emu could show a chain-complete where silicon halts, which is precisely what the guarantee scope statement #2 ("real, staged-with-citation, real-crypto, or provably conservative") claims cannot happen. Smallest fix: after the crafted-tee1 chain leg add `assert not any('mtee_verify' in e for e in emu.events)` (pins the ordering premise dynamically), and either reclassify the row or extend the citation to state it is silicon-equivalent-on-chain-paths rather than conservative.
